DELTA Well-Being Program

Award-winning health and wellness programs

At Nazdar, employee well-being is part of our culture. Through our award-winning DELTA Well-Being Program, we support the physical, emotional, financial, and social well-being of our employees and their families. DELTA, which originated from Diet, Exercise, and Lifestyle Turn-Around, represents our commitment to positive, lasting change and helping employees live healthier lives.

Our commitment to well-being has earned both local and national recognition. Year after year, Nazdar receives recognition through the Cigna Healthy Workforce Designation, the American Heart Association Well-Being Works Better Scorecard, and the Kansas City Business Journal's Healthiest Employers program. Most recently, Nazdar was named one of the 2026 Healthiest 100 Workplaces in America, earning the #35 ranking nationwide for its commitment to employee well-being and exceptional wellness programming.

While well-being is a cornerstone of our culture, we also provide a competitive and comprehensive benefits package designed to support employees through every stage of life.
