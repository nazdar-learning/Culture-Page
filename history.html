Nazdar history

Nazdar was founded in 1922 in the back of a small store in Chicago. Nazdar's transformation from these humble beginnings to one of the world's leading manufacturers of screen printing, flexo and digital inks is the story of the emergence of the screen printing industry itself.

Parent company

Thrall Enterprises

Thrall Enterprises began operating as an independent company in 1972 and has grown organically and through acquisitions. The Company’s main operating subsidiary is Nazdar Company, which includes Nazdar Ltd. in the United Kingdom. In addition to these operating subsidiaries, the Company’s investment portfolio consists of investments in real estate, marketable securities, and minority positions in other operating companies.
