Life at Nazdar

Community

At Nazdar, community involvement is more than just an occasional activity, it’s a lifestyle, one of our core values and an integral part of our environment.

In addition to the many group activities we coordinate and support, we have several employees who invest substantial personal time and resources to programs that support, improve, and enrich the communities in which they live. We believe that employees who invest their time and resources to charitable organizations also enjoy the benefits of personal growth and continued success.

“When you share, there is always enough”

is the philosophy we embrace and encourage throughout our organization.
